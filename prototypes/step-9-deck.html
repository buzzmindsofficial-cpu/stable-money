Why it matters

Repeat is the cheapest growth in the company.

  • Every point of repeat-rate lift monetizes 30L+ installed users at near-zero marginal CAC.
  • Household savings are structurally shifting toward term deposits — 61.6% of deposits; households contribute 59.3% of total deposits (Mar 2026).
  • AUM per user compounds only if the second booking happens.

Sources: case brief [CF]; RBI deposit data [SR].

Speaker notes: This isn't a retention nice-to-have. It's the highest-leverage P&L lever in the company. → What we actually know — and what we refused to invent.

Key insight

"It's a decision-architecture problem,
not a motivation problem."Users don't distrust FDs — the product gives them no reason to return. The first booking ends the relationship instead of starting it.

Early framing corrected: the KPI window is 90 days, but most FDs mature in 1–3 years — maturity-centric thinking misses the window entirely.

Opportunity

"How might we give first-time FD investors a legitimate, welcome reason to re-engage well before their FD matures — one that reinforces trust rather than manufacturing urgency?"…so that continuing to invest feels like the natural next step, not a fresh decision.
Work pre-maturityClarity before persuasionReduce effortSafety at decisionNo FOMO
Speaker notes: Note what's banned: urgency. If the mechanism needs pressure to work, it's the wrong mechanism for a trust product. → Fourteen ideas went through that filter. Two survived.

Solutions

Two concepts. A pair, not alternatives.

Concept A — "The First 90 Days"

Trust Receipt + Savings Pulse + digest. Earns attention through reassurance.

Concept B — "Repeat in Two Taps"

Pre-filled second booking, insurance clarity, liquidity upfront. Converts through ease.

Parked: maturity countdown (wrong window for most users) · Rejected: personalized recommendations (assistance → advice → mis-selling risk)

Speaker notes: A earns the attention; B converts it. Neither works alone. → The math behind picking these two.

Metrics

A North Star that can't be gamed.

  • RR90 = deliberate 2nd bookings ÷ first-time bookers · (T0, T0+90d] · weekly cohorts · passive auto-renewals excluded by charter
  • Tree: leading (Pulse open, visits d8–90, rebook entry) → behavioral (A1→A4 funnel) → product (disclosure-expand, alt-select, fatigue) → business (ΔV, ΔAUM, ΔRev)
  • Guardrails are kill triggers: any breach pauses rollout — even if RR90 climbs
Speaker notes: Because passive renewals are excluded, this metric cannot be moved by inertia or dark patterns — only by users deliberately choosing to invest again. → Exactly what the risk review stress-tested.
